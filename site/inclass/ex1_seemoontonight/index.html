中秋猜燈謎

Lantern Riddle · Counting a “full house”

Combinations 5-card hands Participation exercise

謎面 The riddle

三人同顏一對和，
五張牌裡月婆娑。
花燈若問幾許亮？
請君細數莫猜錯。

Three of one face, and a matching pair — five cards under the Mid-Autumn moon. How many such lanterns can a full deck light?

Decode the riddle first. 「三人同顏」= three cards of the same number. 「一對和」= one pair (two cards of another number). Together that is a full house: three-of-a-kind + one pair.

牌局設定 Deck rules (read carefully)

A standard deck has 52 cards: 4 suits (kinds), and in each suit the numbers run from 1 to 13. So there are 13 ranks, and for each rank there are exactly 4 cards (one per suit). You form a hand of 5 cards. Order does not matter — count with combinations \(C(n,k)\), not permutations.

Think about how to count the hands the riddle describes. No written submission — a few students will be invited to present in class. 中秋快樂 · Happy Mid-Autumn Festival.

謎底 · Solution Solution

The riddle asks for the number of full-house hands (three of one rank + a pair of another rank):

\[ \begin{aligned} N &= C(13,1)\,C(4,3)\,C(12,1)\,C(4,2) \\ &= 13 \times 4 \times 12 \times 6 \\ &= 3744. \end{aligned} \]
  • \(C(13,1)\): choose the rank for the three-of-a-kind.
  • \(C(4,3)\): choose 3 suits out of 4 for that rank.
  • \(C(12,1)\): choose a different rank for the pair.
  • \(C(4,2)\): choose 2 suits out of 4 for the pair.

Optional: among all \(C(52,5)=2\,598\,960\) five-card hands, \(P=\dfrac{3744}{2\,598\,960}=\dfrac{6}{4165}\).

Quick probability facts

  • 0 ≤ P ≤ 1; sample-space probabilities sum to 1
  • Addition: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)
  • Conditional: P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)
  • Independent iff P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)
  • Bayes: revise priors with new data → posteriors
